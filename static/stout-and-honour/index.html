    House Rules

    MOVESTRIKEBLOCKLEG ITTAUNT

    Hold the phone sideways. Drag anywhere on the left half to move. On a keyboard: WASD move, J strike, K block, L or Space leg it, I taunt, Esc pause. Gamepads work too.

    • White flash: block it. Tap BLOCK right before it lands to parry.
    • Red flash: unblockable. Leg it. Dodge at the last second for an Olé.
    • Tap STRIKE three times for a combo. Hold STRIKE for a Hurley Smash that breaks guards.
    • Win the crowd. Variety fills the craic meter. Repeating yourself bores them.
    • When the craic is ninety, tap Unleash Hell.
    • When time is called, the crowd decides the bout.
    • A pint has to settle before you can drink it. Lads will nick it if you dawdle.
